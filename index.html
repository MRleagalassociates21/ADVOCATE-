Documentation & Advisory

Non-Litigation Services

Legal Documentation, Property Services & Advisory

Our non-litigation services focus on legal documentation, property transactions, preventive legal review, registration assistance and advisory services to help clients understand and address their legal requirements outside contested court proceedings.

1

Legal Drafting & Documentation

  • Sale Agreement, Sale Deed, Gift Deed, Will
  • Release Deed, GPA, SPA, Partition Deed
  • Family Settlement Deed, MOU, Rental & Lease Agreements
  • Affidavits, Declarations, Undertakings, Legal Notices
  • Other civil, contractual & property-related documents
2

Property Registration Services

  • Document preparation & scrutiny
  • Kaveri portal document upload assistance
  • Guidance on Sub-Registrar Office (SRO) procedures
  • Registration appointment support
  • Stamp duty & registration fee guidance
  • Verification of document particulars
  • Assistance with registration procedures
Registration is subject to applicable legal requirements, document acceptance and the procedures of the competent registering authority.
3

Khata & Property Revenue Services

  • Khata application and transfer assistance
  • E-Khata related services
  • Khata correction and mutation assistance
  • Property tax documentation
  • Guidance on property record discrepancies
  • Follow-up assistance with relevant authorities
Grant, transfer or correction of records is subject to applicable rules and the decision of the competent authority.
4

Property Title Verification & Legal Due Diligence

  • Ownership and title document verification
  • Title flow and previous sale deed scrutiny
  • Gift, partition, release deed & inheritance documents
  • Encumbrance Certificate (EC) verification
  • Khata, E-Khata, RTC and revenue records
  • Grant and conversion orders (where applicable)
  • Layout plans and development approvals
  • Survey records and property schedules
  • Identification of title defects and legal risks
  • Preparation of property legal opinions
  • Guidance on additional documents/clarifications
The scope and conclusions depend on documents available, records accessible and applicable law.
5

Property Legal Consultancy

  • Pre-purchase document verification
  • Review of sale agreements and transaction terms
  • Examination of ownership and succession documents
  • Guidance on missing title documents
  • Review of property schedules and boundaries
  • Assistance with property transfer documentation
  • Legal guidance on property-related disputes and risks
6

General Legal Consultancy & Contract Review

  • Review and vetting of agreements
  • Contract drafting and review
  • Undertakings and declarations
  • Legal correspondence and notices
  • Documentation for business and commercial transactions
  • Preliminary legal assessment of contractual issues
  • Guidance on legal obligations and procedural requirements
